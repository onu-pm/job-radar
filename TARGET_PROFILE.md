# Target profile — what counts as a match

Edit this file to change what the radar looks for. It is sent to the model with every triage and scoring call.

## Role
- Mid-level **Product Manager** (also: Product Manager II, Technical Product Manager, Product Owner at a product company).
- About 5 years of PM experience (since Dec 2021) plus earlier CX/program and marketing roles.
- "Senior PM" is okay only as a **stretch**, when the JD asks for ≤6 years.
- Not: APM/Associate/Intern, Lead/Principal/Group/Staff PM, Director/Head/VP, product marketing, project/program manager.

## Domain (strongest → weaker)
Tag every role either **HR tech** or **B2B SaaS**.
1. HR tech: HRIS / HRMS / HCM, payroll, EOR / global employment, HR integrations & unified APIs,
   HR workflow automation, compliance / statutory, AI for HR / recruiting / payroll, recruiting / ATS,
   onboarding, benefits, performance, workforce management, background verification, employee finance.
   A company only counts as HR tech if its *product* serves HR teams or the workforce — not just because it has an HR department.
2. B2B SaaS: any other software company selling to businesses.
- Anything else (consumer apps, agencies, IT services, hardware) is out.

## Location
- Remote roles open to India, or to a time-zone band that includes IST (UTC+5:30).
- Remote roles whose required overlap is reachable from IST (Europe / Middle East / APAC hours, e.g. "4h overlap with CET").
- India-based hybrid/onsite roles count **only when the site setting "Include India hybrid/onsite" is on** (off by default).
- Not: US-only, "must be authorized to work in US/UK/EU/Canada" without sponsorship, or 6+ hours of US-hours overlap.
- If it's unclear, keep it and label it "Location unclear".

## Strengths to weigh heavily (from the resume)
- 0-to-1 embedded integrations marketplace inside HRIS platforms (~35,000 employers, India + Middle East)
- Unified API layer on a canonical data model; integration catalog 75 → 175+
- Zapier-like workflow automation inside HRIS; 5X module activation, MAU 10k → 25k, ARR 4X
- Live 4-agent AI assistant (recruitment, payroll checks, onboarding, compliance); eval/accuracy benchmarks
- Statutory compliance workflows pushing payroll data to government/financial systems
- Developer platform for partner self-listing; ecosystem-led sales
