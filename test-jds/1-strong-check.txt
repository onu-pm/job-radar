The Work

We're looking for a Product Manager to own Payouts — how employees and contractors actually get paid across Check's infrastructure. It's a well-scoped, high-trust domain: ACH is live and stable today, and we're gearing up for bigger swings next year. You'll partner closely with a dedicated engineering counterpart, Check leadership, and our partners — software companies across diverse verticals — to keep payouts rock-solid while building what's next.



In this role, you will:

 - Own execution on Payouts end-to-end, from ACH today toward same-day pay and new payment methods

 - Take a well-scoped problem and run with it — you'll be trusted to execute thoroughly and with strong judgment, not just to check boxes

 - Sweat the details: catch edge cases before they ship and hold a high bar for quality, with minimal need for rework

 - Know when to make the call yourself and when to loop in your manager — escalation judgment matters as much as independent execution

 - Partner closely with your dedicated engineering counterpart and cross-functional teams (engineering, operations, partners) to ship reliably

 - Connect directly with Check's partners to understand how payouts show up in their day-to-day, and translate that into product decisions



Many backgrounds could fit this role, but ideal candidates will have some or all of the following:

 - Product management experience in technology companies

 - A track record of thorough, low-iteration execution on well-scoped work — you ship things that don't come back for rework

 - Good judgment about when to escalate versus handle something yourself

 - Payments experience is a plus, but not required — payroll and tax knowledge isn't required either; we consider it learnable

 - Comfort partnering closely with engineering, operations, and external partners to drive a project to completion

 - Willingness to attend our annual 3-day company offsite in the spring
